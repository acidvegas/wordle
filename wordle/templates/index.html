How to play

Guess the word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word. The colors of the tiles show how close your guess was.

WEARY

W is in the word and in the right spot.

PILLS

I is in the word but in the wrong spot.

VAGUE

U is not in the word in any spot.

There is one word for everyone each day. A new one comes out at midnight UTC.

Hard mode: any revealed hints must be used in later guesses. Turn it on in settings before your first guess.

Bots (IRC, Discord, ...) can play your daily game with an API key. The bot is you: its guesses count on your board, stats and leaderboard spots.
